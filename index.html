AstraOS archive

This is the APT archive for AstraOS 0.2.0 Alpha. An installed AstraOS points apt at this address:

deb [signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/astraos-archive-keyring.gpg] \
https://rafikulislammollaq-dev.github.io/astraos stable main

It is served from the apt branch of the repository, and it is assembled by the build and published after a green one. The index lives at dists/stable/main/binary-amd64/Packages.
